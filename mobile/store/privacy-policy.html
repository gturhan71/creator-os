Creator OS — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026
Developer: [DEVELOPER / COMPANY NAME] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]
App: Creator OS (bundle / package ID: com.creatoros.app)

Summary

Creator OS runs on your device. We do not operate a server for the app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not receive, store or sell your data. There are no advertisements, no analytics and no tracking SDKs in the app.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you work with in the app is stored in a database on your phone only: messages and reply drafts, deals, deliverables and proof links, bookings, link bundles, personas, notification settings, and the language you choose. We cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

  • On iOS, the operating system may include app data in your device or iCloud backups according to your own device settings.
  • Your OpenAI key (Pro, see below) is kept in the iOS Keychain / Android Keystore, not in the database.
  • The app ships with fictional sample data so you can try it. It is not real information about anyone.

2. What the app does not do

  • It does not log in to, read, or send from your Instagram, e-mail or any other social account. Replying opens your mail app or Instagram with the text prepared, and you press send yourself.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • It does not create an account with us and does not ask for your name or e-mail.

4. On-device features you trigger

  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a push server.
  • Copy to clipboard happens only when you tap a reply button.
  • Calendar export (Pro) creates a file that you share yourself through the system share sheet.
  • Opening Instagram or your mail app happens only when you tap the button.
  • Siri / launcher shortcuts (Pro) read the same on-device database to answer you; nothing leaves the device for this.

5. Legal bases and your rights (EEA, UK, Turkey, and similar laws)

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You control your data directly: you can edit or delete items inside the app, remove your OpenAI key in Profile, and delete everything by uninstalling the app. For data held by OpenAI, RevenueCat, Google, Apple or Google Play, please contact those companies. Where processing by these services is needed to provide a feature you request, it is based on performing the service you asked for (and, for OpenAI, on you choosing to enter your own key).

You may contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with any privacy question, and you have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

6. Children

Creator OS is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Security

Data lives in your device's app sandbox, and your API key in the system's secure storage. Connections to third parties use HTTPS.

8. Changes

If we change this policy — for example if we add a feature that connects to an outside service — we will update this page and the effective date, and the app will not start sending new kinds of data without the policy describing it.

Özet

Creator OS cihazında çalışır. Uygulama için bir sunucu işletmiyoruz, kullanıcı hesabı yok ve verilerini almıyor, saklamıyor ya da satmıyoruz. Uygulamada reklam, analitik veya izleme yazılımı bulunmaz.

1. Cihazında kalanlar

Uygulamada çalıştığın her şey yalnızca telefonundaki bir veritabanında tutulur: mesajlar ve yanıt taslakları, anlaşmalar, teslimatlar ve kanıt bağlantıları, randevular, link paketleri, persona'lar, bildirim ayarları ve seçtiğin dil. Bunları biz göremeyiz. Uygulamayı sildiğinde kaldırılır.

  • iOS'ta işletim sistemi, kendi cihaz ayarlarına göre uygulama verisini cihaz veya iCloud yedeklerine dahil edebilir.
  • OpenAI anahtarın (Pro, aşağıya bak) veritabanında değil, iOS Anahtar Zinciri / Android Keystore'da saklanır.
  • Uygulama, denemen için kurgusal örnek verilerle gelir; gerçek kişilere ait bilgi değildir.

2. Uygulamanın yapmadıkları

  • Instagram, e-posta veya başka bir sosyal hesabına giriş yapmaz, oradan okumaz, oradan göndermez. Yanıtla düğmesi, hazırlanan metinle posta uygulamanı veya Instagram'ı açar; gönder tuşuna sen basarsın.
  • Rehberine, fotoğraflarına, konumuna, mikrofonuna veya kamerana erişmez.
  • Bizde hesap açmaz; adını veya e-postanı istemez.
